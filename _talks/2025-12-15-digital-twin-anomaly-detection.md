Presentation of a digital twin pipeline that emulates normal and abnormal power behaviour and benchmarks six unsupervised detectors - LOF, One-Class SVM, Isolation Forest, Autoencoder, LODA and Elliptic Envelope - trained on normal operation alone. LOF and One-Class SVM gave the best accuracy-generalization trade-off (F1 = 0.90).
