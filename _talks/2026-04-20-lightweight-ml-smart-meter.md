Presentation of a leakage-free, time-aware evaluation of three lightweight models - Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM and Random Forest - on the Electricity Load Diagrams 2011-2014 dataset with controlled anomaly injection. Random Forest reached an AUC of 0.932, and feature-importance analysis showed that variability and distribution-shape features discriminate better than absolute consumption levels.
