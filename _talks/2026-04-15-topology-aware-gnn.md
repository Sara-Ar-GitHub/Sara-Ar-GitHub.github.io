Presentation of a graph-based detection framework in which the power grid is modelled as a graph and GAT, GCN and GraphSAGE are trained on AC power-flow data generated with pandapower. GraphSAGE reached an F1-score of about 94% and a ROC-AUC close to 0.97, and GNNExplainer was used to identify the buses that drove each detection.
