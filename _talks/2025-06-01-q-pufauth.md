Presentation of Q-PUFAuth, a lattice-based post-quantum authentication and key agreement protocol for smart grids. The talk covered the weaknesses of LAP-SG, the strengthened PUF challenge-response design against cloning and side-channel attacks, and the formal verification of the resulting protocol with ProVerif and in the Real-or-Random model.
