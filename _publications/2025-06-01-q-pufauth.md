The increasing reliance on smart grid technologies necessitates robust authentication and key agreement mechanisms to secure critical infrastructure against cyber threats. Traditional authentication schemes such as LAP-SG provide lightweight authentication but suffer from vulnerabilities, including susceptibility to quantum attacks and weak physically unclonable function (PUF) implementations. In response, this paper presents an enhanced version of the LAP-SG protocol with strengthened security features and improved efficiency. The proposed protocol integrates quantum-resistant cryptographic mechanisms, leveraging lattice-based encryption to withstand attacks from quantum adversaries. It also strengthens PUF challenge-response mechanisms to mitigate cloning and side-channel attacks. A formal security verification is conducted using ProVerif and the Real-or-Random (ROR) model. The approach ensures mutual authentication, forward secrecy, resistance to impersonation and replay attacks, and scalability for large-scale smart grid deployments.
